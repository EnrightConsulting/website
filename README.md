# EnView

> **Know what matters today.**

EnView is an **Asset Intelligence Platform** that transforms information from your home, business, finances, maintenance systems, and connected services into timely, actionable intelligence.

Rather than presenting dashboards full of data, EnView delivers a daily briefing that helps you understand what deserves your attention and why.

---

## Vision

Create the world's most intuitive Asset Intelligence Platform for homes and businesses.

---

## Mission

Transform data into timely, actionable intelligence.

## Product Promise

## Core Principles

- Intelligence over information.
- Everything is an asset.
- Capture once. Use everywhere.
- Every recommendation explains why.

- Generating recommendations
